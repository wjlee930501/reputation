# Re:putation report brand and conversion system

## 1. Atmosphere & Identity
Premium clinic partner proposal and operating review, built as Jinja/WeasyPrint documents. Re:putation is a service of Newvisit, MotionLabs' hospital marketing brand, so the reports borrow the essence of the Newvisit service deck (v52): one orange signal on black, white and neutral grays; headlines with a single orange phrase; a short orange dash above the cover promise; an eyebrow of orange ordinal plus ink label; large light ordinals for lists; 2pt black rules that open and close lists and tables; pure-black statement surfaces where the key figure turns orange; letterspaced caps for the running head and footer. Deck-only devices do not carry over (except the monthly hero's single orange dot, taken from the landing hero): no circles, dots, rounded chips or cards, arrows or full-bleed colour pages. The editorial data-report conventions stay: hairline-ruled modules, large tabular figures on white, a running head with role label and page counter, a full-width footer rule. This is an A4 composition, not a slide deck or SaaS dashboard. Diagnosis answers why now, why Newvisit, what changes. The monthly director report (`doctor_report_v3.html` + `doctor_report_v3.css`) answers, in the landing page's voice, how the clinic did this month, what we did, and what we will do next. Readers: time-constrained clinic owner (not a marketer) first, evidence-seeking AE second (appendix).

## 2. Color
White #ffffff, ink #111111, black #000000 (statement surfaces), signal orange #ff3d00 for marks — bars, rules, the masthead tick, the cover dash, headline emphasis, the delivery figure on black — and deep orange #d13200 for small text and the CTA fill (5:1 on white; #ff3d00 alone is 3.6:1 and fails for 9–11pt). Muted #6b6b6b, faint #a3a3a3 (ordinals, table header labels, running head), rule #dcdcdc, soft surface #f6f6f6. At most one emphasis panel in soft gray per page besides the black surface. Current-month metric and bar are orange, prior month ink. No blue, tinted grays, gradients, shadows, rounded corners or left accent bars on boxes.

## 3. Typography
Bundled embedded Pretendard variable font declared for the 100–900 weight range; weights in use are 800 (display), 700 (headings, labels), 600 (small labels), 500 (ledger titles), 400 (body) and 300 (statement sub-copy). Lead 30pt, monthly conclusion 24pt white on the black hero band (21pt compact), section h1 28pt, identity 20pt, section h2 15pt, proposal/work titles 14pt, body 11pt, spec/process 10.5pt, captions/tables 9–9.5pt, metrics 44pt (monthly 40pt; unavailable 22–25pt muted), monthly good-news facts 20pt, ordinals 20pt, delivery figure 36pt. Tabular numbers, negative tracking on display sizes, Korean word preservation, emergency wrapping for long fields. No text below 9pt. Large figures denote observations or labeled assets, never patients.

## 4. Spacing & Layout
A4, 17mm side rails, 18mm head, 20mm foot; 4/8/12/16/24pt rhythm. Running head: `@top-right` sample/INTERNAL label and `page / pages`; running foot: `@bottom-left` (70%) `NEWVISIT · RE:PUTATION` and `@bottom-right` (30%) `MOTIONLABS INC.` in letterspaced caps over one continuous hairline. Each section opens with a masthead (`Re:putation by Newvisit` left, orange ordinal plus ink label right, ink hairline with a 28pt orange tick at its left end). h2 modules carry an ink rule above the title; content rows use pale hairlines; KPI strips draw a 2pt ink rule above every metric (orange for the current month) with gutters instead of a grid; a gutter grid built from `border-spacing` pulls in by one gutter on each side and widens by two (`width:calc(100% + 2 * gutter)`) so the first and last module land on the rails; tables use a 2pt ink rule under faint letterspaced header labels, no vertical rules and no zebra; the process ledger is opened and closed by 2pt ink rules; the asset grid is topped by orange rules. Three validated main pages plus complete flowing appendix. Diagnosis: promise and identity, white metric pair with mention-share bars, three ruled questions; dark why-now statement and two numbered spec-sheet proposals; ruled asset grid, process ledger, marketer panel, prices and CTA. Monthly: page 1 opens with the landing hero — a black band with an orange dot greeting (“원장님, 이번 달 우리 병원은 어땠을까요?”), period · title, clinic name and the one-sentence plain conclusion in white, plus the reason sentence when the month was not compared; then “AI에게 물었을 때 우리 병원이 나온 비율”, the same-scale prior/current pair, one plain sentence (“100번 물으면 약 N번…”), the denominator caption, three ruled good-news facts (questions where the clinic appeared, articles published with cumulative total, own articles used as AI sources), one gray “다음 달에 할 일” panel and a single short caveat. Page 2 (“이번 달 한 일”): published work ledger, then “그 결과” with one shown and one not-shown answer. Page 3 (“다음 달 계획”): ruled priorities, dark publication ledger, three-step marketer process. No delta line in %p. Appendices: 5pt cell padding, 9.5pt evidence. HTML preview reflows below 600px; PDF is delivery surface.

## 5. Components & Contracts
Shared masthead, statement-band, metrics (with scale/bar), ledger, opportunity-row, proposal + spec rows, asset-grid, process, marketer-followthrough, readout, work-proof ledger, evidence pair, priorities, delivery-ledger, CTA and appendix tables. The internal AE report (`report.html`) includes the same stylesheet with KPI, note, highlight and action-box overrides. Shared tokens, different page rhythms. Monthly bars use 0–100% on a rule-colored track; prior is ink, current is accent; prior/delta require valid matched cohorts. Headline remains pooled LOCAL confirmed repeats; platform averages stay labeled. Content identity/ownership stays upstream; None and zero have distinct labels. Lead payload gains no private fields. Full questions, works, excerpts and methods remain in appendix. Page/row anchors, required text, page markers (`01 / 이번 달 결과`, `02 / 이번 달 한 일`, `03 / 다음 달 계획`, appendix `부록 / 자세한 기록`), links, font and hashes remain mandatory; `doctor_pdf_v3.v3_expectation` and `report_artifact_validation` change in lockstep with the template. Lead becomes lead-v12; doctor-pdf-v3 and legacy routes remain.

## 6. Motion & Interaction
Static document; semantic headings/tables, underlined links and focus outlines. One configured regional-fit CTA. INTERNAL: AE-only every page (running head), no links. Fictional labels every sample page. No contact/token changes.

## 7. Narrative & Surface
Useful information, publication history and baseline accumulate after work starts; no first-mover ranking promise. Existing homepage/blog remain; additional public hub, grounded query content and marketer-explained monthly findings are deliverables. Official facts and authoritative references ground content; no invented strengths. Onboarding sets operating criteria and discusses hospital-owned domains; no per-article manual approval. Regional overlap checked, differentiated positioning may be discussed; no exclusivity/scarcity. Prices remain 60/90/120만원 + VAT, 12/16/20 articles. AI mentions/citations observed, patient growth aspirational. One concise KPI caveat; full limitations in appendix.

## 8. Accessibility Constraints & Accepted Debt
Body 11pt, notes 9pt, clear contrast/status and same-scale bars. Production PDF/PNG, anchor/text/link/font checks and long-name/unavailable/high/internal tests are acceptance evidence. No accepted clipping or omitted evidence. Browser performance/tooling changes do not apply to static PDFs; use existing local dependencies. No production reads/deployment.

## 9. Director copy voice (monthly)
The monthly director PDF speaks like reputation.motionlabs.kr: warm 합니다체 to “원장님”, short plain sentences, no marketing or measurement jargon (비교 보류, 공통 눈금, 확정 관측/반복, 판정 보류, 조합, 소유 URL, 관측, 수행, 약정, 기준선, 코호트, %p, English kickers). Platforms are named ChatGPT and Gemini. Good news is stated only from real counts; unknown is never printed as zero; a month that was not compared says why in one sentence (first measurement, method change, …); a drop is said gently with the next action, and “조금” only for small drops. One caveat that AI answers are not patients and rankings are not promised stays on page 1; the full limitations live in the appendix. All strings live in `report_narrative.py` and `report_engine._director_copy`; `tests/test_report_plain_language.py` bans the jargon and runs every new sentence through the medical-ad filter.

## 10. v4 monthly rhythm (`doctor_report_v4.html` + `doctor_report_v4.css`)
The v4 director report keeps §1–§4 (no rounded corners, no shadows, hairlines, one orange, one black statement surface per page) and adds one page rhythm. **Spacing scale** is a single ladder, 4/8/12/16/24/32/48pt: masthead → page title 24pt; page title → first module 32pt; between modules 32pt (compact 24pt, never lower); module title (h2) → content 12pt; a caption sits 6pt under its element. Side rails stay 17mm from `report_editorial.css @page`; `@page` margins are never changed. **Type scale** (Pretendard): page title 28pt/800; card figures 40pt/800 (stepping to 28/22/20/18pt by estimated string width — Hangul 0.82em, digits 0.65em, % 0.95em, space 0.25em — so any value stays on one line inside its 107pt card, with `overflow-wrap:anywhere` as the last resort; text states 15pt muted — all on one baseline because the KPI strip is a real table whose value row is `vertical-align:baseline`); black-band figure 36pt/800 orange; module title 15pt/700 on a 0.75pt ink rule; row/table titles 11pt/700; body 11pt/400; labels/kickers 9pt/700 letterspaced; captions 9pt/400 muted, line-height 1.4; table cells 9.5pt; nothing below 9pt; numbers tabular. **Accent**: exactly one orange hierarchy per page — the AI 노출률 card on 01, the current month point and the exposure bars on 03, the black band figure on 02/04/06, the exposed case kicker + mark on 05 — key-line and insight labels are muted.

**Readability minimums (2026-11, fixed — no density may lower them)**: bar rows (label 11pt/700 + caption 9pt, line-height 1.35, 7pt padding top and bottom, `height:30pt` as the cell minimum) ≥ 30pt; group-table, heatmap and every other table body row ≥ 22pt (cell padding ≥ 5pt, 6pt on tables); ledger rows (`.rule-row`) 7pt padding; every text line keeps ≥ 4pt from the row's hairline and lines in a row never overlap. `compact` density only shrinks module gaps (32 → 24pt), masthead/title margins and figure sizes (40 → 36, 36 → 32pt). `tests/test_doctor_pdf_v4.py::test_readability_minimums` measures WeasyPrint boxes for the growth, dense and 18×40-char-keyword fixtures in both densities.

**Flow instead of squeezing**: every body section starts on a new page (`break-before:page`, named pages `v4-p1…v4-p6`, `v4-appendix`) and may continue onto further pages. A continuation page carries, in the running head, the full masthead with “(이어짐)” and the page counter — a `position:running()` element shown with `element(masthead-cont, first-except)`, so the section's first page shows the in-flow masthead and its own counter line instead. 01, 05 and 06 (bounded content) and 04 with ≤ 6 replaced keywords are flex columns (`.anchored`, `min-height:726pt`) whose `.spring` seats the closing element (notice + link, 시사점 panel, black band) at the foot; 02, 03 and a long 04 flow as blocks because a flex column cannot fragment. 03's order is trend | AI 서비스별, then 진료 항목별 | 질문 그룹별 (without a trend the pairing 진료 항목별 | 서비스별 + 그룹별), then the heatmap and the 2pt-ruled citation line 32pt below it; if the heatmap does not fit it continues on the next page with the table header repeated. The validator (`doctor_pdf_v4.v4_layout_valid`) accepts 7–14 pages, requires `p1-start` on page 0, `p{n}-start` in increasing page order, each `p{n}-end` on or after its start and before `p{n+1}-start`, `appendix-start` after `p6-end`; every `p*`/`appendix-*`/`kw-row-*` anchor inside the 17/18/20mm rails (table anchors horizontally only, since a table may continue across pages); and the table anchors the view implies (`p2-table`, `p3-heat`, `p4-table`, `p6-table`, `appendix-table` — ids on the `<table>` elements, whose box is the real table width, so a 103% table is rejected) must exist. Because a section's texts may fall on several pages, `v4_expectation` binds each required text to the page it was laid out on (`page_bound_texts`, from the last validated document's boxes, texts missing from boxes such as SVG labels go to the section's first page) and the binary validator re-checks them there with pypdf.

**Heatmap**: one hairline-row system — `table-layout:fixed`, fixed platform columns (56pt single, 44pt 2-up), keyword column the rest, so the table can never pass the rail; keyword cells wrap inside the cell (`white-space:normal; overflow-wrap:anywhere; word-break:keep-all`, never `nowrap`); filled cells span the full row height, text 9.5pt centered (2-up keywords 9pt); up to 10 keywords one full-width table, 11+ two halves (equal counts) as columns of the same table with a 16pt empty gutter column so left and right rows always share a height. Bars everywhere share one track colour (#dcdcdc), one height (6pt) and right-aligned values on a common x; tables right-align numeric columns (`.num`). WeasyPrint notes: a `padding:` shorthand whose first token is `var()` is not expanded (the stylesheet uses longhands wherever a variable sets padding); flex containers drop content when they overflow a page (never flex a section that can grow).
