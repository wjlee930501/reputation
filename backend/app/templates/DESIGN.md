# Re:putation report brand and conversion system

## 1. Atmosphere & Identity
Premium clinic partner proposal and operating review, built as Jinja/WeasyPrint documents. Re:putation is a service of Newvisit, MotionLabs' hospital marketing brand, so the reports borrow the essence of the Newvisit service deck (v52): one orange signal on black, white and neutral grays; headlines with a single orange phrase; a short orange dash above the cover promise; an eyebrow of orange ordinal plus ink label; large light ordinals for lists; 2pt black rules that open and close lists and tables; pure-black statement surfaces where the key figure turns orange; letterspaced caps for the running head and footer. Deck-only devices do not carry over: no circles, dots, rounded chips or cards, arrows or full-bleed colour pages. The editorial data-report conventions stay: hairline-ruled modules, large tabular figures on white, a running head with role label and page counter, a full-width footer rule. This is an A4 composition, not a slide deck or SaaS dashboard. Diagnosis answers why now, why Newvisit, what changes. Monthly answers what was delivered, observed, and comes next. Readers: time-constrained clinic owner and evidence-seeking AE.

## 2. Color
White #ffffff, ink #111111, black #000000 (statement surfaces), signal orange #ff3d00 for marks — bars, rules, the masthead tick, the cover dash, headline emphasis, the delivery figure on black — and deep orange #d13200 for small text and the CTA fill (5:1 on white; #ff3d00 alone is 3.6:1 and fails for 9–11pt). Muted #6b6b6b, faint #a3a3a3 (ordinals, table header labels, running head), rule #dcdcdc, soft surface #f6f6f6. At most one emphasis panel in soft gray per page besides the black surface. Current-month metric and bar are orange, prior month ink. No blue, tinted grays, gradients, shadows, rounded corners or left accent bars on boxes.

## 3. Typography
Bundled embedded Pretendard variable font declared for the 100–900 weight range; weights in use are 800 (display), 700 (headings, labels), 600 (small labels), 500 (ledger titles), 400 (body) and 300 (statement sub-copy). Lead 30pt, monthly conclusion 28pt, section h1 28pt, identity 20pt, section h2 15pt, proposal/work titles 14pt, body 11pt, spec/process 10.5pt, captions/tables 9–9.5pt, metrics 44pt (unavailable 25pt muted), ordinals 20pt, delivery figure 36pt. Tabular numbers, negative tracking on display sizes, Korean word preservation, emergency wrapping for long fields. No text below 9pt. Large figures denote observations or labeled assets, never patients.

## 4. Spacing & Layout
A4, 17mm side rails, 18mm head, 20mm foot; 4/8/12/16/24pt rhythm. Running head: `@top-right` sample/INTERNAL label and `page / pages`; running foot: `@bottom-left` (70%) `NEWVISIT · RE:PUTATION` and `@bottom-right` (30%) `MOTIONLABS INC.` in letterspaced caps over one continuous hairline. Each section opens with a masthead (`Re:putation by Newvisit` left, orange ordinal plus ink label right, ink hairline with a 28pt orange tick at its left end). h2 modules carry an ink rule above the title; content rows use pale hairlines; KPI strips draw a 2pt ink rule above every metric (orange for the current month) with gutters instead of a grid; a gutter grid built from `border-spacing` pulls in by one gutter on each side and widens by two (`width:calc(100% + 2 * gutter)`) so the first and last module land on the rails; tables use a 2pt ink rule under faint letterspaced header labels, no vertical rules and no zebra; the process ledger is opened and closed by 2pt ink rules; the asset grid is topped by orange rules. Three validated main pages plus complete flowing appendix. Diagnosis: promise and identity, white metric pair with mention-share bars, three ruled questions; dark why-now statement and two numbered spec-sheet proposals; ruled asset grid, process ledger, marketer panel, prices and CTA. Monthly: factual conclusion, same-scale comparison with a delta line, delivered/observed readout; portfolio ledger with contrasting positive/negative evidence; ruled priorities, dark fulfillment ledger and next steps. Appendices: 5pt cell padding, 9.5pt evidence. HTML preview reflows below 600px; PDF is delivery surface.

## 5. Components & Contracts
Shared masthead, statement-band, metrics (with scale/bar), ledger, opportunity-row, proposal + spec rows, asset-grid, process, marketer-followthrough, readout, work-proof ledger, evidence pair, priorities, delivery-ledger, CTA and appendix tables. The internal AE report (`report.html`) includes the same stylesheet with KPI, note, highlight and action-box overrides. Shared tokens, different page rhythms. Monthly bars use 0–100% on a rule-colored track; prior is ink, current is accent; prior/delta require valid matched cohorts. Headline remains pooled LOCAL confirmed repeats; platform averages stay labeled. Content identity/ownership stays upstream; None and zero have distinct labels. Lead payload gains no private fields. Full questions, works, excerpts and methods remain in appendix. Page/row anchors, required text, page markers (`01 / 관측과 결론` …), links, font and hashes remain mandatory. Lead becomes lead-v12; doctor-pdf-v3 and legacy routes remain.

## 6. Motion & Interaction
Static document; semantic headings/tables, underlined links and focus outlines. One configured regional-fit CTA. INTERNAL: AE-only every page (running head), no links. Fictional labels every sample page. No contact/token changes.

## 7. Narrative & Surface
Useful information, publication history and baseline accumulate after work starts; no first-mover ranking promise. Existing homepage/blog remain; additional public hub, grounded query content and marketer-explained monthly findings are deliverables. Official facts and authoritative references ground content; no invented strengths. Onboarding sets operating criteria and discusses hospital-owned domains; no per-article manual approval. Regional overlap checked, differentiated positioning may be discussed; no exclusivity/scarcity. Prices remain 60/90/120만원 + VAT, 12/16/20 articles. AI mentions/citations observed, patient growth aspirational. One concise KPI caveat; full limitations in appendix.

## 8. Accessibility Constraints & Accepted Debt
Body 11pt, notes 9pt, clear contrast/status and same-scale bars. Production PDF/PNG, anchor/text/link/font checks and long-name/unavailable/high/internal tests are acceptance evidence. No accepted clipping or omitted evidence. Browser performance/tooling changes do not apply to static PDFs; use existing local dependencies. No production reads/deployment.
